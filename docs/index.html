How it works

Three beats. Zero ceremony.

X-Shot stays out of your way until you need it — then it finishes the job before your next Slack message.

  1. 01

    Select

    Drag a region. Same muscle memory as the system shot — ⇧⌘4 by default.

  2. 02

    Annotate

    Arrows, boxes, highlighter, text, blur. Keyboard shortcuts while you edit.

  3. 03

    Ship

    Clipboard is ready. Keep a library when you need the shot again later.

Why it fits

Made for high-volume screenshotters.

If your day is bug reports, design feedback, support replies, and “can you circle that?” — the friction isn’t capturing. It’s everything after.

  • Annotate in the same breath No bounce to Preview, no “where did that PNG go?”
  • Stay in flow Capture lands in an editor that feels like a quick mark-up desk, not a project.
  • Keep what matters Clipboard for now. Library for the shots you’ll reuse.
